# INT-Q004 — Complete model answer

**Spoken answer:** “I would acknowledge my decision and the information available then, describe the detection and mitigation, and explain the system changes and follow-up owners. I would avoid blaming a person or pretending the incident had one simple cause.”

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">اعترف بقرارك ونطاقه، واذكر ما كانت الأدلة وقتها. وضح mitigation، contributing factors، واختبار أو guard يمنع التكرار. بلا تبرير مطلق أو لوم أشخاص.</p>

**Mechanics:** Google SRE postmortem guidance emphasizes contributing conditions, learning and actionable follow-up. Blameless does not mean actions have no owners or deadlines. [Amazon STAR guidance](https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/interview-loop) and [Google SRE postmortem guidance](https://sre.google/workbook/postmortem-culture/) were checked 2026-10-10. The answer is a coaching model, not the learner’s personal history.

**Common wrong answer:** “Nobody was responsible, so nothing needs to change.” That confuses lack of blame with lack of accountability.

**Follow-up 1:** What if another team also contributed? Describe the interface or process gap and each team’s factual role, keeping the response focused on collaborative correction.

**Follow-up 2:** What if the fix was not completed? State the status and owner honestly; do not claim recurrence was prevented until there is evidence.

**Rubric for self-review:** Keep the story truthful, identify individual and team actions separately, give evidence for the result, name uncertainty, and answer the follow-up without blaming people. This is visible practice, not a scored assessment.
