# INT-Q002 — Complete model answer

**Spoken answer:** “I would use a real decision with a shared goal, describe the competing options and constraints fairly, and explain what evidence changed or confirmed the choice. I would state the team decision, my contribution, and the observed follow-up.”

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">الخلاف مش مسابقة فوز. اشرح الهدف المشترك، البدائل، evidence، طريقة حسم القرار، ودورك. لو رأيك اتغير، ده ممكن يكون علامة حكم جيد.</p>

**Mechanics:** STAR should distinguish what you advocated from what the team adopted. A senior answer can include a reversible experiment, an ADR, rollback path and later review, if those actually happened. [Amazon STAR guidance](https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/interview-loop) and [Google SRE postmortem guidance](https://sre.google/workbook/postmortem-culture/) were checked 2026-10-10. The answer is a coaching model, not the learner’s personal history.

**Common wrong answer:** “The other team was wrong and I convinced them.” This hides constraints and makes cooperation unverifiable.

**Follow-up 1:** What if your option was rejected? Explain why the chosen option better fit constraints, how you supported it, and what evidence would trigger revisiting the decision.

**Follow-up 2:** What if no formal ADR exists? Use a real PR, meeting outcome or test as evidence, and say the written record was limited.

**Rubric for self-review:** Keep the story truthful, identify individual and team actions separately, give evidence for the result, name uncertainty, and answer the follow-up without blaming people. This is visible practice, not a scored assessment.
