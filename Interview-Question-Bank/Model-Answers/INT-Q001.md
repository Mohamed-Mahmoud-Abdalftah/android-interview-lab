# INT-Q001 — Complete model answer

**Spoken answer:** “For my real example, I would state the symptom and my scope, describe the evidence I gathered before naming a cause, explain the mitigation and partners, and give only outcomes I can verify. I would close with the follow-up that reduced recurrence.”

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">اختَر incident حقيقية لك. ابدأ بعرض symptom لا root cause مفترضة، ثم دورك وtimeline والخطوات والنتيجة الموثوقة. لو metric غير متاحة، صرّح بذلك.</p>

**Mechanics:** The structure is Situation, Task, Action, Result, with uncertainty and next steps. A hypothetical sample can guide form but must never be presented as personal history. Blameless practice focuses on contributing conditions and action items. [Amazon STAR guidance](https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/interview-loop) and [Google SRE postmortem guidance](https://sre.google/workbook/postmortem-culture/) were checked 2026-10-10. The answer is a coaching model, not the learner’s personal history.

**Common wrong answer:** “I personally fixed a major outage and cut incidents 80%” when neither responsibility nor number can be supported.

**Follow-up 1:** What did you personally do versus the team? Name your decision, analysis, code or coordination artifact, then name the team’s separate contributions accurately.

**Follow-up 2:** What if the root cause remained uncertain? Say what was ruled out and what evidence remained missing; report mitigation and investigation status without pretending certainty.

**Rubric for self-review:** Keep the story truthful, identify individual and team actions separately, give evidence for the result, name uncertainty, and answer the follow-up without blaming people. This is visible practice, not a scored assessment.
