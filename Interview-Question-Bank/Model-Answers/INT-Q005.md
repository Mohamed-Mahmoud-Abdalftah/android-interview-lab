# INT-Q005 — Complete model answer

**Spoken answer:** “I would identify a real decision I owned, the outcomes and boundaries delegated to others, how I monitored risk and removed blockers, and the final verified result. I would credit team members while being precise about my own accountability.”

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">التفويض لا يعني إنك كتبت كل code، ولا إنك تركت الفريق من غير متابعة. اشرح مَن كان يملك كل جزء، milestones، قرار escalation، والنتيجة الحقيقية.</p>

**Mechanics:** A strong STAR account separates authority, execution and accountability. Evidence can include a plan, review, risk register, release decision or follow-up, but only if it actually existed. [Amazon STAR guidance](https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/interview-loop) and [Google SRE postmortem guidance](https://sre.google/workbook/postmortem-culture/) were checked 2026-10-10. The answer is a coaching model, not the learner’s personal history.

**Common wrong answer:** “I led everything” without identifying decisions, partners or artifacts.

**Follow-up 1:** How would you answer if a teammate’s task slipped? Explain how you discovered the risk, adjusted scope or support, and communicated impact without public blame.

**Follow-up 2:** What if you were not the DRI? Say so directly and frame your contribution as influence, implementation or review rather than ownership of the whole project.

**Rubric for self-review:** Keep the story truthful, identify individual and team actions separately, give evidence for the result, name uncertainty, and answer the follow-up without blaming people. This is visible practice, not a scored assessment.
