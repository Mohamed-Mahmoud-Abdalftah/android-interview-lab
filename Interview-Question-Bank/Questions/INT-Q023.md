# INT-Q023 — Clarifying questions

## Interview question

> **Interviewer:** You are asked to design offline sync. Which few clarifying questions materially change the architecture, and which questions are just delay?

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>السياق:</strong> إجابة خمس دقايق تبدأ تاريخ الموضوع ومتوصلش للقرار، أو ادعاء أرقام وخبرة مش حقيقية.</p>

## Follow-ups an interviewer may ask

1. What is the most common wrong answer here, and what production failure can it cause?
2. What exact test or trace would convince you that the fix works?
3. Under which constraint would you choose a different design?
