# SYS-Q006 — BFS and DFS choice

## Interview question

> **Interviewer:** Given an unweighted social graph, choose BFS or DFS to find the shortest connection path. State the invariant, complexity, and the conditions that would change your choice.

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>السياق:</strong> Dijkstra على negative edge، BFS على weighted graph، أو DP table من غير تعريف معنى الخانة.</p>

## Follow-ups an interviewer may ask

1. What is the most common wrong answer here, and what production failure can it cause?
2. What exact test or trace would convince you that the fix works?
3. Under which constraint would you choose a different design?
