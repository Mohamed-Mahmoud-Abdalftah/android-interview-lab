# INT-Q015 — Post-incident actions

## Interview question

> **Interviewer:** Turn 'be more careful' into owned, testable follow-ups. Which actions change prevention, detection, mitigation, and recovery?

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>السياق:</strong> الفريق يقضي ساعات في root cause بينما crash rate بتزيد وعنده rollback آمن.</p>

## Follow-ups an interviewer may ask

1. What is the most common wrong answer here, and what production failure can it cause?
2. What exact test or trace would convince you that the fix works?
3. Under which constraint would you choose a different design?
