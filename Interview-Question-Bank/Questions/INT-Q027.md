# INT-Q027 — Gap-driven revision

## Interview question

> **Interviewer:** A mock interview exposes weak answers in Binder, Flow cancellation, and migrations. Build a revision loop from the failed reasoning rather than rereading everything.

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>السياق:</strong> المتعلم يقرأ كل الإجابات ويعتبر recognition mastery، أو يعمل mocks كتير من غير تصحيح gaps.</p>

## Follow-ups an interviewer may ask

1. What is the most common wrong answer here, and what production failure can it cause?
2. What exact test or trace would convince you that the fix works?
3. Under which constraint would you choose a different design?
