# STF-Q006 — Migration governance

## Interview question

> **Interviewer:** Five teams must migrate to a new API. How do you version contracts, publish tooling, track adoption, handle exceptions, and retire the old path without a permanent dual system?

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>السياق:</strong> فريق يعيد كتابة التطبيق كله من غير migration path أو success metrics، أو platform team تبني abstraction بلا users</p>

## Follow-ups an interviewer may ask

1. What is the most common wrong answer here, and what production failure can it cause?
2. What exact test or trace would convince you that the fix works?
3. Under which constraint would you choose a different design?
