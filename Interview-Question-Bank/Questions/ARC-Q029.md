# ARC-Q029 — ADR challenge

## Interview question

> **Interviewer:** You disagree with an accepted ADR after new production evidence appears. How do you challenge it constructively, what evidence is enough to reopen it, and how do you keep delivery moving meanwhile?

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>السياق:</strong> Reviewer يطلب pattern أكبر من غير failure حقيقي، أو يوافق على repository بتكتب network result فوق local edits من غير conflict policy.</p>

## Follow-ups an interviewer may ask

1. What is the most common wrong answer here, and what production failure can it cause?
2. What exact test or trace would convince you that the fix works?
3. Under which constraint would you choose a different design?
