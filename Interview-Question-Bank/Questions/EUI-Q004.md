# EUI-Q004 — Custom layout

## Interview question

> **Interviewer:** Design a custom two-child Compose Layout. Explain constraints, measurement rules, placement, intrinsic measurements, RTL, and what happens if you measure a child twice.

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar"><strong>السياق:</strong> custom component سريعة بصريًا لكن semantics مفقودة، أو state تضيع لما layout تتحول list-detail</p>

## Follow-ups an interviewer may ask

1. What is the most common wrong answer here, and what production failure can it cause?
2. What exact test or trace would convince you that the fix works?
3. Under which constraint would you choose a different design?
