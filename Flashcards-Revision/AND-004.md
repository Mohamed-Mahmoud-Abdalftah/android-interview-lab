# AND-004 revision cards

1. **Looper and MessageQueue:** The Looper processes one queued message at a time. A long Runnable prevents later input, lifecycle, and frame work from running until it returns.
2. **Synchronous Binder call:** A synchronous Binder call blocks the caller. From main, queueing, remote contention, or nested locks can exceed the response budget even when average service time is low.
3. **ANR evidence:** Classify whether the frame is running, blocked, waiting on Binder, or waiting on a lock. Follow ownership and compare repeated traces and timeline evidence.
4. **Handler leak:** A queued callback captures the Activity or view until execution or removal. Tie callbacks to a lifecycle owner, remove them, or move state work to a longer-lived owner without capturing views.
5. **Main-safe API contract:** At the data boundary that knows the driver is blocking, so callers receive a main-safe suspend contract. Do not scatter dispatcher guesses through UI code.
