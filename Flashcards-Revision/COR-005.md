# COR-005 revision cards

1. **collectLatest cancellation:** It cancels the previous collector action and waits for its cancellation before running the next. Blocking or already-committed external work may continue, so I do not treat cancellation as rollback.
2. **buffer versus conflate:** Buffer lets the producer get ahead until capacity fills, preserving queued values. Conflate lets a slow collector skip intermediates and process the latest. I use conflate only when intermediate progress has no business meaning.
3. **flatMapLatest search:** I normalize input, debounce UI noise, remove equal queries, and switch to the latest search Flow. I still handle cancellation cooperation, cache policy, errors, loading state, and stale writes outside the cancellable pipeline.
4. **Stale result generation:** Because cancellation is cooperative and external work can complete after cancellation. A monotonically increasing generation lets the owner accept only results for the current request.
5. **Backpressure contract:** I classify each value: durable business event, diagnostic sample, or replaceable state. Durable events need persistence and acknowledgement; samples can use a documented drop policy with counters; state can conflate to latest.
