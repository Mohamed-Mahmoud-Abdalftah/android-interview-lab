# PRO-005 revision cards

1. **Measurement question:** Name the user journey, metric, percentile, device class, build, and competing cause before collecting data.
2. **Startup modes:** Cold creates process, warm may reuse process, and hot reuses Activity; compare matching scenarios.
3. **Perfetto trace reading:** Follow main thread, binder, I/O, scheduling, and frames in one timeline rather than isolated logs.
4. **Memory evidence:** Distinguish allocation churn, retained objects, native memory, and process pressure before calling a leak.
5. **Incident rollout:** Use cohort/version metrics, a reversible flag or rollback, and privacy-safe diagnostics.
