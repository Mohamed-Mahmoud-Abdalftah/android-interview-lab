# COR-002 revision cards

1. **Child failure in `coroutineScope`?** Fails the parent and cancels siblings after propagating a non-cancellation exception.
2. **Child failure in `supervisorScope`?** Does not automatically fail siblings; the failure still needs explicit handling.
3. **Parent cancellation under supervision?** Cancels all children.
4. **Can `CoroutineExceptionHandler` recover a child?** No; recovery belongs around the operation or in a designed supervised result.
5. **Why is broad `runCatching` risky?** It catches `CancellationException`; rethrow it before mapping failures.
6. **Interview decision rule:** Choose Job topology from atomic versus partial product semantics.
