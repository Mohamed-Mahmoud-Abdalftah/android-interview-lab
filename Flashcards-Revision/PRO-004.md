# PRO-004 revision cards

1. **Build pipeline:** Explain source to class files, DEX, packaged artifact, signing, installation, and ART execution without merging the stages.
2. **Compatibility matrix:** Verify Gradle, AGP, Kotlin, JDK, and Compose compiler compatibility from current official tables.
