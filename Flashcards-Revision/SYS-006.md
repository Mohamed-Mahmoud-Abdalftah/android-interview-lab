# SYS-006 revision cards

1. **Requirement discovery:** Clarify users, latency, offline, consistency, privacy, abuse, and failure priorities before components.
2. **State ownership:** Every mutable state has one authority, update path, persistence level, and conflict policy.
3. **Concurrency boundary:** Protect the smallest shared transition and avoid holding locks across slow external work.
4. **Alternative comparison:** Compare consistency, latency, complexity, cost, and operational recovery rather than naming a winner.
5. **Design verification:** Use invariants, failure injection, load model, observability, and staged rollout to test assumptions.
