# INT-002 revision cards

1. **Severity and scope:** Quantify affected users, functions, versions, geographies, and data/security impact.
2. **Mitigation before certainty:** Use a reversible rollback or flag when impact is material, while preserving evidence for diagnosis.
3. **Incident roles:** One owner coordinates decisions, communications, operations, and a shared factual timeline.
4. **Hypothesis testing:** Choose the next observation that distinguishes competing causes and timebox branches.
5. **Post-incident actions:** Actions change code, tests, detection, or process and include owner, deadline, and proof of completion.
