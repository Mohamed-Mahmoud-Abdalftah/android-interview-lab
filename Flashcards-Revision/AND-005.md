# AND-005 revision cards

1. **Work mechanism selection:** Choose from lifetime and user visibility. Durable deferred upload fits WorkManager; an active user-visible transfer may justify FGS; screen-only work can stay in its owner scope.
2. **Worker retry policy:** Permanent authentication or validation failures will loop and waste battery. Classify transient transport failures, cap attempts, use backoff, and expose terminal state.
3. **Foreground-service boundary:** The work is ongoing and user-visible, the app is allowed to start it in that context, it declares the correct type, and it posts the required notification promptly.
4. **Doze and exact timing:** Background scheduling is inexact and Doze batches work. Clarify the real user outcome, use push or approved exact mechanisms only when policy permits, and design stale-data UX.
5. **Unique work and progress:** A stable unique name plus KEEP, REPLACE, or APPEND policy defines deduplication or sequencing. Choose from whether old work remains valid and persist progress outside ephemeral callbacks.
