# INT-005 revision cards

1. **Twenty-second answer:** Give the decision and one reason without losing the key boundary.
2. **Ninety-second depth:** Add mechanism, concrete example, failure path, and trade-off.
3. **Clarifying questions:** Ask about scale, lifetime, consistency, and constraints before overcommitting.
4. **Uncertainty language:** Separate what you know, infer, and would verify without bluffing.
5. **Shadowing review:** Record, compare to a written structure, remove fillers, and repeat with a new example.
