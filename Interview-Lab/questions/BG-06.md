# BG-06

## Original interview question

What are Doze mode and app standby restrictions?

## What the interviewer evaluates

Explain Doze and app standby as OS power controls affecting background timing.

## Explanation in Arabic

ما هو وضع الخمول (Doze Mode) وقيود وضع الاستعداد للتطبيقات (App Standby Buckets) في Android؟ وكيف تؤثر على المهام الخلفية؟

1. وضع الخمول (Doze Mode - أُضيف في Android 6.0):
   - **متى يعمل**: عندما يترك المستخدم الهاتف ثابتاً على الطاولة، والشاشة مطفأة، والهاتف غير موصول بالشاحن.
   - **ماذا يفعل النظام (Restrictions)**:
     * يقطع الوصول للشبكة بالكامل عن التطبيقات في الخلفية.
     * يعلق الـ JobScheduler و WorkManager والـ Sync Adapters.
     * يتجاهل الـ WakeLocks لمنع استنزاف المعالج.
     * يعلق تنبيهات AlarmManager العادية.
   - **نوافذ الصيانة (Maintenance Windows)**:
     * يفتح النظام نافذة صيانة دورية كل فترة (تتباعد تدريجياً: بعد 10 دقائق، ثم ساعة، ثم عدة ساعات) لتشغيل المهام المؤجلة ومزامنة البيانات دفعة واحدة (Batching)، ثم يعود للخمول العميق.

2. وضع الاستعداد للتطبيقات (App Standby Buckets - أُضيف في Android 9.0):
   - يصنف النظام التطبيقات إلى 5 مجموعات بناءً على مدى استخدام المستخدم لها:
     * **Active**: التطبيق مفتوح الآن أو قيد الاستخدام الفعلي -> لا قيود.
     * **Working Set**: يستخدمه المستخدم يومياً -> قيود خفيفة جداً.
     * **Frequent**: يستخدمه بانتظام أسبوعياً -> قيود على عدد المهام والـ Jobs المسموحة يومياً.
     * **Rare**: نادراً ما يفتحه المستخدم -> لا يُسمح بتشغيل Jobs إلا مرة واحدة كل 24 ساعة!
     * **Restricted**: التطبيق يستهلك موارد مفرطة -> حظر كامل تقريباً في الخلفية.

3. كيف يتعامل مهندس Android المحترف مع هذه القيود؟
   - تصميم التطبيق ليكون متسامحاً مع التأجيل (Delay-Tolerant).
   - استخدام FCM High-Priority Push Notifications للرسائل العاجلة لإيقاظ التطبيق مؤقتاً.
   - عدم محاولة التحايل على النظام بحيل مشبوهة تؤدي لرفض التطبيق في متجر Google Play.

## Practical walkthrough

## Why it matters and how it works

Power policy varies by Android version, device state and app usage bucket. Do not infer that plugged-in or foreground status always gives identical behavior on every OEM. Test with official adb/device tools and observe actual last-success rather than scheduled time.

## Worked example

Conceptual state model; UI shows freshness instead of assuming periodic work ran at a fixed second.

## Trade-offs and decision criteria

Respecting Doze saves battery and system health but increases background latency. Expedited work and foreground services have quotas/restrictions and must match user-visible urgency. Server push may notify, but offline/reconnect catch-up still required.

## Failure modes

A design that promises sync every minute while the device sleeps will fail under power policy. Repeated wake locks or exact alarms for routine polling abuse battery and may be restricted. Treat missed scheduled windows as normal, not data corruption.

## Production perspective

A field app records edits locally and shows Pending while device is idle; WorkManager syncs when permitted and on next app foreground. Metrics track pending age and server acknowledgement so product knows when delay exceeds expectations.

## Interview answer — short

Doze mode suspends network access, jobs, and alarms when the device is unplugged, stationary, and screen-off, executing work only during periodic maintenance windows. App Standby Buckets throttle background tasks based on user engagement frequency (Active, Working Set, Frequent, Rare), restricting rare apps to at most once-daily job executions.

## Interview answer — structured

Doze Mode and App Standby Buckets represent Android's core OS power management architecture designed to maximize battery life.

First, **Doze Mode (Device-Level Power Policy)**. Next, enters **Light Doze** shortly after screen-off, restricting network and deferring jobs.
Transitions into **Deep Doze** when stationary and unplugged. Disables network access entirely, suspends JobScheduler and WorkManager tasks, ignores standard WakeLocks, and defers standard alarms. Executes deferred work only during periodic, increasingly spaced **Maintenance Windows** (e.g. 10m, 30m, 1h, 2h).
**App Standby Buckets (App-Level Engagement Policy)**. Categorizes apps into tiers based on recency and frequency of user interaction.
With Active, app is currently in foreground or running a foreground service. A Working Set is used daily; minimal restrictions.
A Frequent is used regularly but not daily; jobs throttled to intervals. Rarely opened; jobs capped at once every 24 hours.
Finally, with Restricted, extreme background limits, zero background network jobs.


## Interview answer — deep senior

Architectural Strategies to build resilient apps under Doze and Standby Buckets.

First, with Design for Delay-Tolerance, assume background synchronization can be delayed by 2–4 hours. When the user returns to the foreground, trigger a proactive refresh in `onStart()` or `repeatOnLifecycle`. Next, with FCM High-Priority Push for Time-Critical Events, high-priority Firebase Cloud Messaging grants a temporary Doze exemption window and network ticket, allowing the app to display a critical notification or run a short expedited job.
Additionally, with Testing Doze Mode via ADB, senior engineers verify background behavior in CI and local testing using ADB commands. Force Doze: `adb shell dumpsys deviceidle force-idle`
Inspect Standby Bucket: `adb shell am get-inactive <package>` Finally, set Bucket: `adb shell am set-standby-bucket <package> rare`


## How to answer in the interview

- **Why:** OS protects battery by deferring work.
- **How:** design for delay and observable pending state.
- **Trade-off:** battery policy versus freshness.
- **Failure case:** deadline assumes device wakes on time.
- **Production solution:** server reconciliation and delayed-work monitoring.

## Deep-dive talking points

Explain delay versus data loss, foreground catch-up, user-visible urgency and device tests.

## Follow-up questions

- **Interviewer:** "Can an app request battery optimization whitelist exemption (`REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS`)?"
  **Answer:** "Yes, but Google Play policy strictly limits this permission to specialized use cases (health monitoring, VoIP, navigation); violating it leads to app removal."

- **Interviewer:** "Does AlarmManager `setExactAndAllowWhileIdle()` fire during Deep Doze?"
  **Answer:** "Yes, but the OS limits how frequently it can wake the device (at most once every 9–15 minutes) and network access remains restricted."
