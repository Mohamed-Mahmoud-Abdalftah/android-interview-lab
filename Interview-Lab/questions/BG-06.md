# BG-06

## Original interview question

What are Doze mode and app standby restrictions?

## What the interviewer evaluates

Explain Doze and app standby as OS power controls affecting background timing.

## Concept and technical explanation

Doze mode and App Standby are battery-saving power management systems introduced in Android 6.0 (API 23) and enhanced in subsequent releases to drastically curb background battery consumption. Doze mode activates when a device is unplugged from power, stationary, and has its screen turned off for an extended period, operating in two stages: Light Doze (restricting network access and deferring jobs during brief screen-off phases) and Deep Doze (suspending network access, ignoring wake locks, deferring JobScheduler tasks, and silencing standard alarms). Deep Doze periodically opens brief maintenance windows to process batched work. App Standby categorizes apps into standby buckets (Active, Working Set, Frequent, Rare, Restricted) based on recency of user engagement, restricting background job frequency accordingly. WorkManager is fully compliant with both mechanisms.

## Practical walkthrough (English)

Adapting background work for Doze mode and App Standby:
```kotlin
// 1. Regular WorkManager tasks: Automatically deferred until Doze maintenance windows
val regularSyncRequest = OneTimeWorkRequestBuilder<SyncWorker>()
    .setConstraints(Constraints.Builder().setRequiredNetworkType(NetworkType.CONNECTED).build())
    .build()
WorkManager.getInstance(context).enqueue(regularSyncRequest) // Waits for maintenance window

// 2. Expedited Work: Executes out-of-quota even during Doze for time-sensitive tasks
val criticalMessageRequest = OneTimeWorkRequestBuilder<SendMessageWorker>()
    .setExpedited(OutOfQuotaPolicy.RUN_AS_NON_EXPEDITED_WORK_REQUEST)
    .build()
WorkManager.getInstance(context).enqueue(criticalMessageRequest)

// 3. Exact user alarm: Penetrates Doze mode strictly for alarms (e.g., wake-up alarm)
fun setDozePenetratingAlarm(context: Context, triggerEpochMs: Long, pi: PendingIntent) {
    val am = context.getSystemService(AlarmManager::class.java)
    am.setExactAndAllowWhileIdle(AlarmManager.RTC_WAKEUP, triggerEpochMs, pi)
}
```

## Explanation in Arabic

ما هو وضع الخمول (Doze Mode) وقيود وضع الاستعداد للتطبيقات (App Standby Buckets) في Android؟ وكيف تؤثر على المهام الخلفية؟

1. وضع الخمول (Doze Mode - أُضيف في Android 6.0):
   - **متى يعمل**: عندما يترك المستخدم الهاتف ثابتاً على الطاولة، والشاشة مطفأة، والهاتف غير موصول بالشاحن.
   - **ماذا يفعل النظام (Restrictions)**:
     * يقطع الوصول للشبكة بالكامل عن التطبيقات في الخلفية.
     * يعلق الـ JobScheduler و WorkManager والـ Sync Adapters.
     * يتجاهل الـ WakeLocks لمنع استنزاف المعالج.
     * يعلق تنبيهات AlarmManager العادية.
   - **نوافذ الصيانة (Maintenance Windows)**:
     * يفتح النظام نافذة صيانة دورية كل فترة (تتباعد تدريجياً: بعد 10 دقائق، ثم ساعة، ثم عدة ساعات) لتشغيل المهام المؤجلة ومزامنة البيانات دفعة واحدة (Batching)، ثم يعود للخمول العميق.

2. وضع الاستعداد للتطبيقات (App Standby Buckets - أُضيف في Android 9.0):
   - يصنف النظام التطبيقات إلى 5 مجموعات بناءً على مدى استخدام المستخدم لها:
     * **Active**: التطبيق مفتوح الآن أو قيد الاستخدام الفعلي -> لا قيود.
     * **Working Set**: يستخدمه المستخدم يومياً -> قيود خفيفة جداً.
     * **Frequent**: يستخدمه بانتظام أسبوعياً -> قيود على عدد المهام والـ Jobs المسموحة يومياً.
     * **Rare**: نادراً ما يفتحه المستخدم -> لا يُسمح بتشغيل Jobs إلا مرة واحدة كل 24 ساعة!
     * **Restricted**: التطبيق يستهلك موارد مفرطة -> حظر كامل تقريباً في الخلفية.

3. كيف يتعامل مهندس Android المحترف مع هذه القيود؟
   - تصميم التطبيق ليكون متسامحاً مع التأجيل (Delay-Tolerant).
   - استخدام FCM High-Priority Push Notifications للرسائل العاجلة لإيقاظ التطبيق مؤقتاً.
   - عدم محاولة التحايل على النظام بحيل مشبوهة تؤدي لرفض التطبيق في متجر Google Play.

## Why it matters and how it works

Power policy varies by Android version, device state and app usage bucket. Do not infer that plugged-in or foreground status always gives identical behavior on every OEM. Test with official adb/device tools and observe actual last-success rather than scheduled time.

## Trade-offs and decision criteria

Respecting Doze saves battery and system health but increases background latency. Expedited work and foreground services have quotas/restrictions and must match user-visible urgency. Server push may notify, but offline/reconnect catch-up still required.

## Failure modes

A design that promises sync every minute while the device sleeps will fail under power policy. Repeated wake locks or exact alarms for routine polling abuse battery and may be restricted. Treat missed scheduled windows as normal, not data corruption.

## Production perspective

A field app records edits locally and shows Pending while device is idle; WorkManager syncs when permitted and on next app foreground. Metrics track pending age and server acknowledgement so product knows when delay exceeds expectations.

## Interview answer — short

Doze mode restricts network access, ignores wake locks, and defers background jobs and alarms when a device is unplugged, stationary, and screen-off, opening brief maintenance windows periodically to process batched work. App Standby places unused apps into buckets like Working Set, Frequent, and Rare, limiting their job and alarm execution frequency. WorkManager natively complies with both power systems by deferring normal jobs while supporting expedited work for urgent tasks.

## Interview answer — structured

Doze mode and App Standby are Android's primary platform mechanisms designed to prevent rogue background processes from depleting battery life.
Doze mode activates when a device is disconnected from power, stationary, and left with its screen turned off. It operates across two phases: Light Doze applies initial network restrictions while allowing job execution, while Deep Doze engages when the device remains stationary for an extended period. Under Deep Doze, the system suspends all network access, ignores CPU wake locks, defers standard JobScheduler tasks, and suppresses ordinary alarms. Periodically, the operating system opens brief maintenance windows to execute queued network requests and pending jobs before re-entering deep sleep with progressively widening intervals.
App Standby evaluates individual application usage patterns independently of device state. It classifies apps into App Standby Buckets—Active, Working Set, Frequent, Rare, and Restricted—based on how recently and frequently the user interacts with the app. Apps in lower buckets face severe daily limits on JobScheduler execution time and restricted network access.
WorkManager natively harmonizes with both power systems: standard workers defer until maintenance windows or bucket allowances open, while urgent tasks can leverage Expedited Work quotas to execute promptly.

## Interview answer — deep senior

Under the hood, understanding Doze mode transitions is essential for diagnosing delayed background processing in enterprise applications.
When a device transitions into Deep Doze, the Linux kernel enters low-power sleep states. If an application holds a Partial WakeLock, Android ignores the lock to preserve power. Furthermore, network access is completely blocked at the iptables firewall layer for all applications except those with whitelisted high-priority push exemptions.
During the periodic maintenance windows, the system unblocks network access and triggers JobScheduler to flush accumulated jobs. However, each successive maintenance window is scheduled farther apart (e.g., 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, up to several hours), meaning non-critical background synchronization can be delayed for hours while the user sleeps.
Developers often attempt to bypass Doze mode by requesting battery optimization whitelisting via the REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS permission. However, Google Play strictly prohibits this permission for general-purpose apps, reserving it exclusively for core tracking, health monitor, or VoIP applications; misuse results in immediate store rejection.
Instead, staff engineers architect applications around platform compliance: routine tasks run as standard WorkManager requests awaiting maintenance windows, time-critical messages utilize high-priority Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) data pushes that grant temporary Doze exemption windows, and true user-facing clock alarms utilize AlarmManager's setExactAndAllowWhileIdle.

## Follow-up questions

- **Interviewer:** "Can an app request battery optimization whitelist exemption (`REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS`)?"
  **Answer:** "Yes, but Google Play policy strictly limits this permission to specialized use cases (health monitoring, VoIP, navigation); violating it leads to app removal."

- **Interviewer:** "Does AlarmManager `setExactAndAllowWhileIdle()` fire during Deep Doze?"
  **Answer:** "Yes, but the OS limits how frequently it can wake the device (at most once every 9–15 minutes) and network access remains restricted."
