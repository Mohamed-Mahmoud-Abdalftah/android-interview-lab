# Day 46 optional speaking — truthful ownership story

Read [INT-004](../09-Leadership-English/int-004-senior-ownership-and-behavioral-star.md). This is guided practice, not an assessment or audio analysis. Fill the [evidence worksheet](../09-Leadership-English/STAR-EVIDENCE-WORKSHEET.md) with your own facts before using a personal example.

**Pacing:** “The situation was… / My responsibility was… / I chose… because… / The verified result was… / One thing I would change is…” Keep each pause short and concrete.

**About 20 seconds:** “I can describe a real decision by separating my responsibility from the team’s work, naming the evidence I used, and stating only the result I can verify.”

**About 90 seconds, fill only from your own history:** “In [real context], the risk was [observable problem]. My role was [scope]. I considered [actual options] and chose [decision] because [evidence]. I personally [actions], while the team [their work]. We observed [verified result and source]. The main uncertainty was [limit], so I [follow-up].” If a field cannot be supported, say that plainly instead of inventing it.

**Useful phrases:** “I was responsible for…”, “The team contributed…”, “The evidence available at the time…”, “I do not have a reliable production metric”, “We changed the process by…”. Avoid blaming colleagues.

**Self-check:** Can you answer “What did you personally do?” and “How do you know it worked?” without adding a fact you cannot verify? Use [revision cards](../Flashcards-Revision/INT-004.md). Sources: [Amazon interview loop](https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/interview-loop) and [Google SRE postmortems](https://sre.google/workbook/postmortem-culture/), checked 2026-10-10.
