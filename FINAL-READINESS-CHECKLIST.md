# Readiness checklist (guideline, not a hiring guarantee)

Use after studying the core, not as a Day 1 test. Record examples from your own work rather than invented achievements.

- [ ] Explain Kotlin/Android behavior from a causal trace and state the contract versus implementation detail.
- [ ] Implement and test a Kotlin solution with edge cases and defensible complexity.
- [ ] Diagnose lifecycle, cancellation, data, build, and performance issues with evidence.
- [ ] Compare at least two mobile designs, including offline/error/security/observability boundaries.
- [ ] Show reliable release and incident decisions, including rollback and monitoring.
- [ ] Give a concise 20-second and detailed 90-second English explanation without reciting a script.
- [ ] Tell factual senior ownership and collaboration stories with honest metrics.

Optional timed mock packs and scoring require explicit opt-in. Strong performance across varied rounds is useful evidence of preparation, not a prediction of a hiring result. [Current scope](ISSUES-AND-LIMITATIONS.md) remains limited to the first batch.
